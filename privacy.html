This is a personal website run by Ken Reid. It has no ads, no paywalls, no user accounts, and nothing to sell. I do not sell, rent, or trade any information about visitors, and I collect as little as the site can get away with. Here is the full picture.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 so I can see roughly how many people visit and which pages they read. Google Analytics sets cookies (names starting with _ga) to tell returning visitors apart from new ones, and collects things like your approximate location (city level), browser, device type, and the pages you view. I see this data only in aggregate; I cannot identify individual visitors from it, and I have no interest in trying.

Google processes this data on its own infrastructure under its privacy policy. If you would rather not be counted, you can block analytics with any content blocker (uBlock Origin works well), use your browser's tracking protection, or install Google's own opt-out browser add-on. The site works identically either way.

Cookies and local storage

Beyond the Google Analytics cookies above, the site sets no cookies of its own. It does use your browser's local storage for two small preferences:

  • kr-theme remembers whether you picked dark or light mode.
  • kr-nerd remembers whether you switched on nerd mode.

These never leave your browser and contain nothing about you. Clearing your browser data removes them, and the site falls back to its defaults.

Comments

Blog posts use giscus for comments, which stores them as GitHub Discussions. Reading comments requires nothing from you. Writing one requires signing in with a GitHub account, at which point your comment and GitHub username are public on both this site and GitHub, governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Hosting and third-party requests

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's servers handle every request and may log IP addresses as described in GitHub's privacy statement. I never see those logs.

A few pages also talk to other services directly from your browser:

  • The photo map loads map tiles from CARTO, built on OpenStreetMap data.
  • The contact page fetches my recent posts from Bluesky's public API.
  • Full-size photographs load from GitHub's servers when you open one in the gallery.

Each of those services sees your IP address for that request, the same as visiting any website does. Fonts, scripts, and styles are all served from this site itself, not from third-party CDNs. Links to other sites (social profiles, the "Buy me a coffee" button, and so on) load nothing until you click them; where they lead is governed by each destination's own privacy policy.

Email

If you email me at ken@kenreid.co.uk, I keep the email so I can reply and remember what we talked about. I do not add you to any list, and there is no newsletter run from this site. My Substack is a separate service with its own privacy policy.

Offline caching

The site uses a service worker to cache pages and images in your browser so repeat visits are faster and pages you have read work offline. That cache lives on your device, is managed by your browser, and sends nothing anywhere.

Changes

If the site starts doing something new with data, this page changes and the date at the top moves. Questions or concerns are welcome at ken@kenreid.co.uk.
