Research, Live

A paper is a poor way to show a moving thing. Each part of this series takes one piece of my published research, rebuilds it as something running in your browser, and hands you the parameters. Some of these took a cluster and six months the first time. They now fit in a page you can open on a phone, which says more about the last decade of hardware than about the work.
